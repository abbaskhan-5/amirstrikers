About the Team

Amir Strikers is a cricket team built on friendship, discipline and a hunger to win. Our squad has strong batsmen, sharp bowlers and match-winning all-rounders.

Our owners back every player with kit, training and match opportunities. If you love cricket and want to play with a serious team, register and we will call you for trials.
